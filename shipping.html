Shipping & Returns

Delivery options, timelines and how to return an item

Last updated: January 2026

Shipping

Delivery areas

We currently deliver within Nigeria. Delivery to specific states or cities may vary; we will confirm coverage when you place your order or contact us on WhatsApp.

Processing time

Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days after payment confirmation (or after we confirm the order if payment is arranged offline). During peak periods or promotions, processing may take a little longer.

Delivery time

  • Major cities — often 2–5 business days after dispatch
  • Other locations — typically 3–7 business days after dispatch

These are estimates only. Delays can occur due to courier issues, weather, or incorrect address details. You will receive tracking or status updates where available.

Shipping costs

Shipping fees depend on location and order size. Free shipping may apply on orders above a stated threshold (currently shown at checkout as free over ₦50,000 where that promotion is active). The exact shipping cost for your order is calculated or confirmed before final payment.

Order tracking

Once your order is dispatched, we may share a tracking reference or update you via WhatsApp or phone. You can also message us with your order ID for a status update.

Failed delivery

If delivery fails because of an incomplete or incorrect address, or if no one is available to receive the parcel after reasonable attempts, we may contact you to rearrange. Extra delivery fees may apply for re-delivery.

Returns & Exchanges

Return window

You may request a return or exchange within 14 days of receiving your order, subject to the conditions below.

Eligible items

  • Items must be unused, unopened and in original packaging where possible
  • Fragrance, body sprays and personal-care products that have been opened or used are generally not eligible for return for hygiene and safety reasons, unless the product is defective or we sent the wrong item
  • Clothing and accessories must be unworn, unwashed and with tags attached

Defective or incorrect items

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and your order details. We will arrange a replacement or refund as appropriate.

How to start a return

  1. Contact us via WhatsApp (9064578588) or email with your order ID and reason for return
  2. Wait for our confirmation and return instructions
  3. Send the item back as directed (we may provide a return address or pickup arrangement depending on location)

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, approved refunds are processed within a reasonable time (typically 5–10 business days) to the original payment method or another method agreed with you. Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Exchanges

Exchanges for size or alternative products depend on stock availability. Contact us to arrange an exchange under the same eligibility rules as returns.

Questions

  • Email: Kylievicky422@gmail.com

Our delivery zones, fees and return rules are designed to support a smooth customer experience and can be updated as your operations expand.
